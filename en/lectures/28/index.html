Lecture 28

Integrals in geometry and physics

What this lecture does

A partition divides a geometric or physical quantity into finite pieces. Their sums identify the integrand before any antiderivative is chosen. We apply this method to area, volume and length, then to work and to the mass and moments of one nonuniform rod.
